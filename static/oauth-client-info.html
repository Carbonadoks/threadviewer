ATProto Codex Bluesky OAuth Client

Matrix Feed Authorization

This OAuth client is used by the ATProto Codex matrix feed viewer to connect a Bluesky account and read the feeds needed for the authenticated matrix display.

What It Reads

Your profile, feed preferences, following timeline, and selected custom feed data.

What It Does Not Do

It does not post, like, repost, or change account settings.
